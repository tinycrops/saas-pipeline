# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name
SaaS Pipeline AI Builder

## Vision
Empower solo founders and small teams to build, launch, and scale profitable SaaS businesses from zero to $100k/month by leveraging AI, niche focus, and affiliate-driven growth. The product provides a modular, automated toolkit that guides users through every step of the SaaS journey.

## Goals
- Enable rapid identification of high-value SaaS opportunities in underserved niches.
- Automate the process of prototyping, validating, and iterating on SaaS products.
- Build scalable affiliate and user growth engines with gamification.
- Use AI to continuously improve the product based on real user feedback.
- Foster transparency and trust by encouraging building in public.

## Target Users
- Solo founders
- Indie hackers
- Small SaaS teams
- Niche creators and operators

## Key Features
1. **Niche Research Module**
    - Interview workflow for identifying pain points in a chosen niche
    - Positioning statement generator
2. **Prototype Builder**
    - Feature selection and MVP design guidance
    - Branding inspiration and first-use experience tips
3. **Customer Value & Affiliate Terms**
    - LTV calculator
    - Affiliate revenue share recommender
4. **Affiliate Engine**
    - Creator list builder (B- and C-list)
    - Personalized DM generator
    - Affiliate tracking and dashboard
5. **Gamification**
    - Leaderboards, badges, streaks, and progress bars for affiliates and users
    - Automated updates and spotlights
6. **AI Co-Creation**
    - Feedback summarization
    - Feature suggestion and prioritization
7. **Building in Public**
    - Artifact sharing and update generator
    - Trust-building workflow

## User Stories
- As a founder, I want to quickly discover a painful problem in a niche I understand, so I can build something valuable.
- As a builder, I want to create a magical MVP with a killer feature and great branding, so my product stands out.
- As a marketer, I want to recruit and incentivize affiliates, so I can grow my user base rapidly.
- As a user, I want to feel rewarded and engaged, so I keep coming back and sharing the product.
- As a product owner, I want to use AI to analyze feedback and suggest improvements, so I can keep churn low and value high.
- As a community member, I want to follow the product journey and trust the builder, so I feel invested in its success.

## Success Metrics
- Number of validated niche opportunities identified
- Time to MVP launch
- Affiliate signups and engagement rates
- User retention and streaks
- Number of product improvements driven by AI feedback
- Social engagement and public artifact shares

## Technical Requirements
- Python 3.8+
- Modular codebase (one file per major function)
- Support for integration with AI APIs (e.g., OpenAI)
- Simple CLI or web interface for workflow automation (future)
- Extensible for additional modules or integrations

## Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk:** Low-quality niche selection → **Mitigation:** Require multiple interviews and pain point validation
- **Risk:** Affiliate fraud or low engagement → **Mitigation:** Gamification, tracking, and milestone rewards
- **Risk:** Feature bloat → **Mitigation:** Prioritize based on user feedback and AI suggestions

## Out of Scope (for v1)
- Full-featured web UI
- Payment processing
- Advanced analytics
